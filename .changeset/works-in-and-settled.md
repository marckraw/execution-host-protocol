Add where a start in a checkout works, `config.worksIn`, and settling a session, a patch's `settled` (MAR-5673). Both are additive.

**`config.worksIn`** is `"auto" | "checkout" | "worktree"` (`EXECUTION_WORKS_IN`), for a start whose `workingDirectory` is in a person's own git checkout. `auto`, the default, is the checkout when no session is working in it, else a worktree of its own. `checkout` is the checkout only while nobody else works there: a host never puts two sessions in one checkout, and refuses such a start with a 409 coded `checkout-taken` naming who (`ExecutionCheckoutTakenRefusal`, `readExecutionCheckoutTakenRefusal`), starting nothing; the client throws it as `ExecutionStartWorksInError` coded `checkout-taken`, with `workingThere` and status 409. `worktree` is always a worktree. A session is working in a checkout while its turn runs, a task of its runs or something is queued for it, unless it was settled.

- A host advertising `start.worksIn.v1` takes it, and its 201 says where the session works: `worksIn: { place, checkoutRoot, workingThere }` (`ExecutionWorksInEcho`, read with `readExecutionWorksInEcho`), `workingThere` being the ids of the sessions working in the checkout as it started.
- A value outside the three is refused as `invalid-payload`.
- The client refuses `checkout` and `worktree`, unsent, on a host without the capability: `ExecutionStartWorksInError`, coded `works-in-unsupported` (`hostTakesWorksIn`). `auto` goes to any host. `ExecutionStartResult`'s `started` carries `worksIn` when the host said it; an echo it can't read is named in `warnings`. The error crosses a `MessagePort` as its class. A project session's workspace echo says where it works, `place` (`checkout` or `worktree`), in snapshots and lists; one this build can't read is left out.

A snapshot and a listed session say `settled: true` while the host holds a session settled, which a new turn of its ends. **`settled`** on `ExecutionSessionPatchRequest` is a boolean: the person settled the session, or took that back. On a host advertising `sessions.settled.v1`, a settled session works in no checkout until a turn of its starts again. The answer echoes it; settling a settled session, or taking back a settle that isn't there, changes nothing and answers the same. Taking back the settle of a session with work running or queued, while another session works in its checkout, is refused with a 409 coded `checkout-taken`, a new `ExecutionSessionPatchRefusalCode`, changing nothing. The client refuses it, unsent, on a host without the capability: `ExecutionSessionPatchError` coded `unsupported-on-host` (`hostTakesSettled`).

The conformance suite has three cases for them, over every carrier, for a host that gives it `checkouts` (`ExecutionHostConformanceCheckouts`: a checkout's folder, and `holdTurns`): a free checkout and a worktree as asked, with their echoes; a checkout another session works in refused as `checkout-taken` naming it, and `auto` given a worktree; and a settle, twice, said by the snapshot and the list, freeing the checkout, refused taken back beside that start, and taken back twice once their work is done. A host that advertises `start.worksIn.v1` must give the hook, and fails the cases without it; only a host that places none skips them. To say what it advertises, `ExecutionHostConformanceHost` now takes `capabilities` (what its `/health` says), required: a suite's caller adds it.
